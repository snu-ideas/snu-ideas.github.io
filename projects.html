Ongoing projects

Three active projects form the foundation of our research plan.

NRF

LLM Agent · Intelligent Hull Form Design

2026–2030 · 5-year project

An engineering agent that designs hull forms through function calling, a geometry kernel and CFD surrogate models.

KRISO

AI-based Hull Form Design Support

2026–2028 · 3-year project

Generative AI-based hull form design technology within a joint system with CFD and maneuverability modules, with industry feedback.

KEIT

Digital Outfitting Design Automation

2023–2027 · Subtask 4

Reinforcement-learning equipment arrangement and optimal pipe routing combined with a digital knowledge base.
